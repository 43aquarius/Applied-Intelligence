# Design philosophy: "Signal Through Quiet Structure"

A visual philosophy for the SAGE architecture figure.

## Manifesto

The figure should read the way a well-run laboratory reads: every
instrument in its place, every label written once and written precisely.
Structure carries the meaning; color confirms it. The composition follows
the left-to-right sweep of the data itself, because the reader's eye
should travel the same road the tokens travel. Space is not emptiness but
punctuation: bands of pale tone separate stages the way silence separates
sentences.

Form is flat and geometric, with corners softened just enough that the
modules feel printed rather than machined. There is no depth, no shadow
beyond a whisper of edge, no ornament. The craft shows in alignment: every
module shares one corner radius, one border weight, one label size, one
grid rhythm. Precision of this order is invisible when present and
unbearable when absent, and it must appear labored over, because it was.

Color speaks in a library voice. Three pale ground tints mark the three
stages of the pipeline - input, cache with its gates, and output - each
desaturated to near-whisper levels, drawn from one cool spectrum so no
module shouts. A single accent, a restrained coral reserved for the
eviction decision and the SAGE-specific mechanisms, marks exactly where
the paper's contribution lives; nothing else may wear it. Arrowheads are
small, dark, and quiet; lines take the shortest honest path and never
cross a module they do not serve.

Typography is the smallest part of the drawing and the most disciplined.
Every label is set in one humanist sans, in one of two sizes: module
identity or module detail. Labels name things ("Confidence Score s_i",
"Layer-adaptive budget b_l"); they never explain them - explanation
belongs to the caption and the text. Words appear only where the eye
needs an anchor, and nowhere else.

The whole must survive three tests: printed in grayscale the stages still
read; reduced to a thumbnail the flow direction still reads; read by a
reviewer in five seconds, the three contributions (scoring, budgets,
reservoirs) still find the eye. This is meticulous, expert work - the
product of someone at the top of the field who knows that a figure is an
argument, not a decoration.

## Subtle reference

Quiet systematic observation: the figure borrows the visual language of a
laboratory bench diagram - annotated, partitioned, calm - so a reader
intuitively reads it as a measured system rather than an illustration.
